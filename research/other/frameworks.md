Cold Outreach Key Patterns

1. Email Length
- Most experts recommend short emails (<75 words)
- Reduces friction and improves reply rates

2. Follow-ups
- Majority of replies come after follow-ups
- 3–5 touchpoints are common

3. ICP Targeting
- Narrow targeting performs better than broad outreach
- Relevance matters more than volume

4. CTA Style
- Simple CTAs perform better:
  - "Worth a quick chat?"
  - "Open to exploring this?"

5. Personalization
- Light personalization works best at scale
- Over-personalization is inefficient
