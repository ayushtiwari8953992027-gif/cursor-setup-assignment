Why You Don't Really Want to Vibe Code It Yourself. 90%+ of The Time.

I love vibe coding. I have built 10+ production apps with it. I spend 1.5-2 hours a day on it. The apps have gotten 750,000+ uses.

But here is what I have learned: you probably do not want to vibe code the things you actually depend on for your business, if there is anything reliable, proven, you can buy yourself.

The other day Riverside, our podcast recording platform, went down right before a recording session with Harry Stebbings and 20VC. We scrambled to Restream as a backup. It worked, but the experience was rough. Upload finished with no clear confirmation. The UI was not built for our workflow.

And the thought hit me: what if I had vibe coded our own podcast recording tool?

I probably could have. WebRTC connection, audio/video capture, file upload pipeline. Weekend project on Replit.

But then what? What happens when one participant's connection drops mid-session? When Chrome updates break the WebRTC implementation? When someone is on a VPN that blocks peer-to-peer connections? Riverside has an engineering team handling hundreds of edge cases I would never think of until they break live on air.

Here is the framework I use now:

1️⃣ Vibe code these: 

- Internal tools nobody else has built
- Prototypes and MVPs
- Workflow glue between two systems
- Things where "good enough" is good enough

2️⃣ Do not vibe code these: 

- Anything with real-time reliability requirements. 
- Anything touching lots of external APIs. 
- Anything truly mission critical. 
- Anything regulated.

The best founders buy or rent 90% of their tools and build the 10% that gives them a genuine edge. 

Vibe coding makes that 10% more accessible than ever. But it also makes it tempting to build the other 90%. That is a trap.

When Riverside went down, we had a bad 20 minutes. If I had built our own tool, I would still be debugging it.

Use vibe coding for your superpowers. Use other people's products for everything else.

The #1 Thing to Understand About AI SDRs: They Can't Figure It Out For You. I.e., 10x times zero is still ... zero.

That is the single most important thing to understand about AI SDRs, AI agents, AI GTM tools. All of them.

Every vendor will tell you to "just turn it on." It does not work that way. Not yet.

At SaaStr, we run 20+ AI agents. They generate over $1M in revenue. We went from 20+ employees to 3 humans plus AI agents. Revenue growth flipped from -19% to +47% year over year.

But every one of those agents is running a playbook we already knew worked. At least that we'd proven with humans a little bit.

The AI SDR that booked a six-figure sponsorship meeting at 6:02 PM on a Saturday? It was running sequences we had tested and refined with humans first. The $70,000 deal closed with zero human involvement? It followed a qualification and pricing framework we built over years.

👉 The AI made it 10x faster and 10x more consistent. But it did not invent the playbook.

If your human SDRs do not know your ICP, your AI SDR will not either. If your sequences do not convert with humans sending them, they will not convert with AI sending them. If you do not have a clear pricing and qualification framework, the AI will just be confidently wrong at scale.

Step 1: Get the playbook working with humans
Step 2: Document every step
Step 3: Feed it to the AI
Step 4: Let the AI run it 24/7 at 10x volume

Skip steps 1 and 2, and you get 10x times zero
