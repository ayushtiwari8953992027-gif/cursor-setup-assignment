# Cold Outreach Playbook

## 1. Positioning & ICP

**Goal:** Who we target and why they care.

- Ideal customer profile (role, company size, trigger)
- Pain / language to speak to
- Who to avoid (bad-fit signals)
- Positioning one-liner

## 2. Offer & Value Proposition

**Goal:** What we lead with in outreach.

- Core offer (outcome, not features)
- Proof points (case study, metric, social proof)
- Why now (urgency / timing)

## 3. Channel Strategy

**Goal:** Where outreach happens and how channels work together.

- Primary channel (e.g. email, LinkedIn)
- Supporting channels
- Seeds / Nets / Spears mix (referrals vs inbound vs outbound)
- When to double down vs switch

## 4. Message Framework

**Goal:** How each touch reads.

- Email / message length
- Subject line / opener pattern
- Personalization level (light vs deep)
- CTA style (low-friction ask)
- Example templates

-

## 5. Sequence & Follow-ups

**Goal:** Cadence from first touch to reply or stop.

- Touch count (e.g. 3–5)
- Timing between touches
- Angle change per follow-up
- Stop rules / disqualify criteria

-

## 6. Systems & Specialization

**Goal:** Make outreach repeatable, not heroic.

- Roles (who prospects, who closes, who onboards)
- Tools / CRM hygiene
- GTM checklist before scaling volume
- AI usage (draft / research vs judgment)

-

## 7. Metrics & Feedback Loop

**Goal:** Know what is working.

- Leading metrics (sends, opens, replies, meetings)
- Lagging metrics (opportunities, revenue)
- Review cadence
- What to change when metrics stall

## 8. Common Mistakes

**Goal:** Failure modes to avoid.

- Broad targeting / weak ICP
- Over-personalization at scale
- Scaling before the human playbook works
- Copy-paste without adapting to context
